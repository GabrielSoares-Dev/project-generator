## Context

Decisions on desktop and phone (design boards 4 and 6).

## User story

As the owner, I want to decide from my phone, so that the team never waits for me to sit down.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given decisions, then each card shows its kind, project, age and the actions the backend allows.
- Given an escalation, then both sides' arguments and the evidence show, with the decision buttons.
- Given an action, then the card leaves the list when the stream confirms it.
